Questions, Answered

Are the free printables really free?

Yes. Every free sample on this site downloads instantly as a PDF with no email signup and no payment. Print as many copies as you like for personal use.

What paper should I print on?

Regular US Letter copy paper works for planners, trackers, and tracing sheets. For coloring pages used with markers, a heavier paper or cardstock prevents bleed-through. Wall art prints best on cardstock or photo paper.

Can I share the files with friends or my classroom?

The free samples are for personal use — printing copies for your own household or classroom is fine. Please don't re-upload or redistribute the files themselves; just share a link to the page instead.

How do I get the full packs?

The complete packs (the 15-page Christmas coloring book, the 4-page Family Planner Pack, the 8-page Toddler Activity Pack, the 10-print Wall Art Pack, and the Spreadsheet Template Bundle) are sold as instant downloads on Payhip. Product links on this site will point to the live listings.

Do I need a special printer?

No. Everything is designed for a standard home printer. Print at 100% scale ("actual size") rather than "fit to page" so the layouts come out exactly as designed.

What's the difference between the free samples and the paid packs?

The free samples are single pages — a taste of each product. The paid packs are the complete sets: more pages, more designs, and companion sheets that work together as a system.
